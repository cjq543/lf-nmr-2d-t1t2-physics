# Data Policy and Asset Provenance Statement

1. **Publicly Redistributed Code, Frozen Weights, and Derived Scalar Archives**:
   All synthetic IR-CPMG simulation scripts (`sim2d.py`), neural architectures (`train2d_v2.py`, `train2d.py`, `dlemlr_model.py`), untouched frozen weights (`v2_refine.pth`, `seed3`, `r = 0.9648`) alongside exploratory wide-grid SR rock checkpoints (`e9_v2rock_seed{1,2,3}.pth`), classical 2D ILT solvers (`baseline2d.py`, `adaptive_lambda_baseline.py`, `sparse_l1_baseline.py`), ablation/evaluation scripts (`eval_synthetic_benchmark.py`, `e1_sign_ablation.py`, `e2_lambda_protocol.py`, `e2b_peak_err_table1.py`, `e3_subsampling.py`, `e6_snr_robustness.py`, `e7_component_robustness.py`, `label_sensitivity.py`), statistical/cross-material/archival analysis scripts (`stats_and_1d2d.py`, `run_hardening_benchmarks.py`, `e8_cross_material.py`, `e8_sr_alignment.py`, `e9_rock_retrain.py`, `eval_historical_strict_odd_even.py`), and sanitized derived JSON tables in `results_archive/` contain zero raw experimental time-domain waveforms, zero binary vendor spreadsheets, and zero local filesystem paths.

2. **Restricted Raw 23 MHz Laboratory Recordings (`13` Liquid Samples)**:
   - Experimental 2D IR-CPMG and paired standalone 1D CPMG/IR acquisitions (`9` edible oils and `4` chemical solvents) were measured on the benchtop 23 MHz Niumag analyzer at University of Shanghai for Science and Technology (experiments performed by Le Liu).
   - **Development-exposure disclosure**: Neural network weights (`v2_refine.pth`) were trained solely on synthetic data (`sim2d.py`), whereas the 13 real 23 MHz acquisitions informed synthetic prior support bounds, diagnosed `v1`'s collapse during `v1->v2` architecture evolution, and were inspected under λ = 10<sup>−3</sup> prior to the systematic `e2` regularization sweep; real-data evaluations in the paper are strictly retrospective transfer checks.
   - Because ~2-year-old original edible oil aliquots are no longer physically recoverable for retrospective bottle-matched GC-FID assays, the `n = 9` fatty-acid branch is explicitly archived as **exploratory literature-label analysis** supported by whole-pipeline exact 9! permutation (`results_archive/nested_loo_exact_permutation.json`) and offline Monte Carlo Gaussian label-noise sensitivity testing (`label_sensitivity.py`, `results_archive/label_sensitivity.json`, `results_archive/label_sensitivity_v2_stats.json`, `results_archive/e4_fingerprint.json`, and `results_archive/stats_and_1d2d.json`).
   - Raw vendor instrument exports (`.pea`, `.ircp`, `.irp`, `.Inv`, and commercial software `output.xls`) are withheld from open redistribution; all derived spectral features, paired solver metrics, matched-capacity `19,393`-parameter 3-seed mechanism ablations (`results_archive/mechanism_ablations.json`), frozen-`v2` physical-mismatch stress sweeps (`mechanism_ablations.json`), front-end precision/rank audits (`results_archive/frontend_precision_sensitivity.json`), exact 9! permutation statistics, nested LOO regression results, paired 1D-vs-2D error tests, real-data odd–even echo split stability / strict odd-calibrated and even-aligned `Odd → Even` forward fit NRMSE / `B=200` (`B=40` BRD) conditional quadrature-noise Monte Carlo sensitivity / non-ILT time-domain parametric fit references including external 2.0 MHz pure n-dodecane (`results_archive/real_uncertainty_and_standards.json`), and vendor `output.xls` boundary audits are preserved in `results_archive/` (`all_samples_features.json`, `v2_real_eval.json`, `e4_fingerprint.json`, `adaptive_baseline.json`, `sparse_l1_baseline.json`, `stats_and_1d2d.json`, `hardening_results.json`, `nested_loo_exact_permutation.json`, `frontend_precision_sensitivity.json`, `real_uncertainty_and_standards.json`, `mechanism_ablations.json`, `archive_audit_and_wood_probe.json`, `label_sensitivity.json`, `label_sensitivity_v2_stats.json`, `vendor_xls_audit.json`, `dlemlr_fair_audit.json`).
   - Note on `results_archive/vendor_xls_audit.json`: The archived JSON comes from the axis-verified `(it1, it2)` audit (`col 0` = *T*<sub>1</sub> `124.520` / `155.052 ms`, `row 0` = *T*<sub>2</sub> `80.309` / `100.000 ms`, directly verified against spreadsheet `Projection Data` and `Spectrum Infos` integrals), matching Supplementary Note S5 and Table S7 of the *Measurement Science and Technology* manuscript.
   - **Post-Freeze Retrospective 23 MHz Workstation-Archive Cohort (`n = 11` Porcine Liver–Lard Phantom Mixtures + Auxiliary Single Cases)**: Evaluated after all model weights and prior bounds were frozen (`zero` development exposure), covering `n = 11` porcine liver–lard phantom mixtures (`LiverLard_01–11`, paired with standalone complex 1D IR and 1D CPMG acquisitions), `Lard_01`, and `Std_C57H98O12_2022`. Raw vendor `.1.k.pea`, `.1.pea`, `.1.irp`, `.1.ircp`, and `output.xls` files are withheld from public redistribution; anonymized per-sample relaxation summaries, solver comparisons, and paired Wilcoxon/Spearman statistics under strict zero-even-leakage `Odd → Even` calibration are preserved in `results_archive/historical_strict_odd_even_eval.json` and verified by `eval_historical_strict_odd_even.py` (matching Manuscript Section 4.4, Supplementary Note S8.4, and Table S13).

3. **Restricted External 2.0 MHz Porous-Rock & Wood Datasets (`40` Rock Facies + `n = 8` Wood `IRCPMG` Probes)**:
   - External 2.0 MHz Magritek Saturation-Recovery–CPMG rock datasets (sandstone, coal/shale, carbonate facies) and `n = 8` longest-window (`4000`-echo) 2.0 MHz wood `IRCPMG` scans were acquired on a 2.0 MHz Magritek platform accessed through **Changzhou Institute of Technology (`常州工学院`, co-author Can Liang)**.
   - Raw Magritek `data2.csv` / `acqu.par` / raw binary waveform exports, as well as **per-record external sample identifiers (core IDs, acquisition dates, raw filenames) and per-record external rows**, are excluded from this public repository; only anonymized cohort-level summary bounds (`results_archive/e8_cross_material.json`, `results_archive/e8_sr_alignment.json`, `results_archive/e9_rock_retrain.json` alongside synthetic `H1`, and `results_archive/archive_audit_and_wood_probe.json`) are retained in `results_archive/` with co-author C. Liang's approval.

4. **Offline Synthetic Reproducibility**:
   - The 200-sample synthetic benchmark in `eval_synthetic_benchmark.py` and ablation scripts (`e1_sign_ablation.py`, `e2_lambda_protocol.py`, `e2b_peak_err_table1.py`, `e3_subsampling.py`, `e6_snr_robustness.py`, and `e7_component_robustness.py`) are fully deterministic from fixed NumPy seeds (`20261112 + 100` for test; `20261112` for dev) and run standalone on a single CPU thread without GPU hardware or external datasets.
